# ESP32-Based E-Paper Device Comparison

> **Note:** The 'de-link' hardware platform is not made as a finished product, and likely will never be sold as one without continued community support. This sheet only hopes to summarize the current (as of March 2026) market of similar devices. Italicized entries describe the traits of the "default" BOM that constitutes the first prototype of a fully constructed "de-link" device. All of these italicized entries are flexible depending on how the user chooses to build around the PCB.
